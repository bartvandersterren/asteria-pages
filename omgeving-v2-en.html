Your base in North Limburg

Hotel Asteria sits on the edge of Venray, right by the A73. In a few minutes you're in the woods of the Maasduinen, in Overloon or in Venray town centre, and within half an hour in Arcen or Venlo. In the evening the wellness area on the top floor awaits, followed by dinner in the brasserie.

A day in four moments

This is what a day looks like when you stay with us and head out to explore.

  1. The breakfast buffet opens at 7 am on weekdays and 8 am at weekends. Ask reception what's on that day.

  2. Cycle through the woods, visit the Overloon War Museum or stroll around Venray. Your car is parked at the hotel and the A73 is just around the corner.

  3. On the Top Floor you'll find 300 m² of wellness with four saunas, a steam room and a plunge pool. Just the thing before dinner.

  4. In the brasserie we cook with regional produce and whatever the season brings. Afterwards it's only a few steps to your room.

Frequently asked questions

Any questions? Call reception: +31 478 511 466

How far are the days out from the hotel?

Most places on this page are within half an hour's drive. Venray town centre is six minutes by car, the Overloon War Museum nine and ZooParc Overloon eleven. Each card shows the drive time from the hotel.

Can I park at the hotel?

Yes, you park at the hotel and you're on the A73 in no time. Reception will happily tell you where best to park at each attraction.

Can I walk or cycle from the hotel?

Yes. Walking and cycling routes start close to the hotel, and the woods of the Maasduinen are a quarter of an hour away. Ask reception about routes and a place for your bike.

Can the hotel arrange tickets for a day out?

For the Overloon War Museum there is a package with entry, dinner and an overnight stay. For other days out, reception will point you in the right direction; just call +31 478 511 466.

What can I do nearby when it rains?

Visit the Overloon War Museum, the Limburgs Museum in Venlo or the Hertog Jan Brewery in Arcen. Back at the hotel, the wellness area on the Top Floor awaits.

How far ahead should I book?

Book your room directly with the button on this page and you'll see straight away which dates are free. At weekends and in the holidays it pays to book early.
