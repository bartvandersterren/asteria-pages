A day of golf

From the first tee to dinner.

  1. A hearty breakfast, then a quarter of an hour's drive to Geijsteren.

Questions about golf

Which courses are near the hotel?

Golfclub Geijsteren, about a quarter of an hour's drive, and Golfbaan Landgoed Bleijenbeek in Afferden, about 25 minutes.

What's included in the golf package?

A green fee for 18 holes at Geijsteren or Bleijenbeek, a three-course dinner, a night in a Royale room and the breakfast buffet.

How do I book the golf package?

With the Book the package button on this page. The code GOLF is filled in automatically.

Do I need to book a tee time myself?

Ask when you book or at reception how tee times are arranged; it differs per course.

Which course should I choose?

If you like woodland with water at the end, choose Geijsteren. If you're after rolling terrain, water features and a castle ruin, Bleijenbeek is for you.

Can I book just a room?

Yes, the booking button at the top books a room without a package. You then arrange the green fee with the course yourself.
