Stories from North Limburg

In the autumn of 1944 there was heavy fighting around Venray. In Overloon you'll learn more about it than anywhere else in the region. If you'd rather see everyday life from the past, head to Museum Village De Locht or the Limburgs Museum in Venlo. Arcen Castle Gardens and the Hertog Jan Brewery are on the same side of the Maas and fit nicely into one afternoon.

A day full of stories

From breakfast to dinner, with a museum in the middle.

  1. Breakfast at the hotel, then a nine-minute drive to Overloon. Arrive early and the museum is still quiet.

  2. Allow a few hours for the halls of vehicles and the stories of local people. A cup of coffee in the museum is part of it.

  3. On the way back you pass the British war cemetery in Venray. A short stop that stays with you.

  4. Back at the hotel, first the wellness area, then dinner in the brasserie.

Questions about culture

Which museum is closest to the hotel?

The Overloon War Museum, nine minutes by car. Museum Village De Locht in Melderslo is about a quarter of an hour away.

How much time do I need for the Overloon War Museum?

Allow half a day to a full day. The museum is large and there's plenty to read and see.

Can I do the castle gardens and the brewery in one day?

Yes. Arcen Castle Gardens and the Hertog Jan Brewery are both in Arcen, about 25 minutes from the hotel.

Is there a package with a museum visit?

Yes, the War Museum package: an overnight stay, museum entry with coffee or tea, a three-course dinner and the breakfast buffet.

What can I do when it rains?

The museums are a good choice in the rain. The Limburgs Museum in Venlo and the Overloon War Museum have plenty of indoor galleries.

Do I need to buy tickets in advance?

That depends on the museum and the season. Check the museum's website or ask reception.
