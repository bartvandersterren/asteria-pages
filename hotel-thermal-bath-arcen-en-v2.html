Your hotel in Venray, close to Arcen

Hotel Asteria is at Maasheseweg 80a in Venray, 23 minutes by car (about 24 km) from Thermaalbad Arcen. You stay in a four-star hotel right on the A73, park free of charge at the door and have our own wellness area with four saunas on the top floor.

Spend the day at the thermal baths in Arcen, have dinner in our brasserie in the evening and finish with the sauna. Hotel Asteria is an independent hotel in Venray and is not part of Thermaalbad Arcen. Tickets for the thermal baths are sold by Thermaalbad Arcen itself.

  • 23 minutes by car to Thermaalbad Arcen
  • Own wellness area, 300 m² with four saunas

Frequently asked questions

Location and baths

How far is Hotel Asteria from Thermaalbad Arcen?

23 minutes by car, about 24 kilometres. The hotel is at Maasheseweg 80a in Venray, right on the A73.

Is Hotel Asteria part of Thermaalbad Arcen?

No. Hotel Asteria is an independent hotel in Venray and is not part of Thermaalbad Arcen. Tickets for the thermal baths are sold by Thermaalbad Arcen itself.

What else is there to do near Arcen?

The castle gardens Kasteeltuinen Arcen, the Hertog Jan brewery and De Maasduinen National Park are 21 to 25 minutes from the hotel.

The hotel

Does the hotel have its own wellness area?

Yes, at the top of the building: 300 m² with four saunas, a steam cabin and a plunge and herbal bath. Hotel guests pay € 12.50 per person. In the wellness package and the winter package the wellness area is included.

When is the wellness area open?

Monday to Thursday from 4 pm to 10 pm, Friday to Sunday from 12 noon to 10 pm. Reserve your time in advance at reception. On request we switch the saunas on earlier for you.

Can I park and charge my car at the hotel?

Yes. Parking at the hotel is free, right on the A73 (exit 8, Venray-Noord). There are four charging points for electric cars and a locked bike shed for your bicycle.
