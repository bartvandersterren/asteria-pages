How to make a day of it

An example with ZooParc Overloon in the middle.

  1. After breakfast you're at ZooParc Overloon in eleven minutes. Arrive early: that's when the animals are most active.

  2. Walk through the themed areas and take your time with your favourite animals.

  3. Back at the hotel, the saunas, steam room and plunge pool on the Top Floor await.

  4. Round off with dinner made with regional produce, then off to your room.

Questions about a day out

Which day out is closest to the hotel?

Mind Mystery in Horst is ten minutes' drive away, ZooParc Overloon eleven.

How far is Toverland?

Toverland in Sevenum is about half an hour's drive from the hotel.

Can I still use the wellness area after a day at the thermal baths?

Yes. Back at the hotel, the wellness area on the Top Floor awaits, with four saunas, a steam room and a plunge pool. Ask reception about opening times.

Is ZooParc Overloon open all year?

Opening times vary by season. Check the park's website before you go.

Can I check in late after a day out?

Let reception know if you'll be arriving later and we'll plan for it. Just call +31 478 511 466.

How do I book a room for my day out?

Choose a room above or use the booking button. You'll see straight away which dates are free.
