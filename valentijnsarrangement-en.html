What does the Valentine's package include?

A night in a Deluxe room with its own infrared sauna, romantically decorated and with a loving surprise. In the evening a 3-course tasting menu at Restaurant Asteria with a glass of prosecco, the next morning an extensive breakfast buffet and late check-out until 13.00 (subject to availability). Parking is free.

When can we come?

The Valentine's package is for Valentine's weekend: arrival on Saturday 13 or Sunday 14 February 2027, for one night. The promo code VALENTIJN is already filled in for you.

How much does the package cost?

You will see the price when you book, as soon as you choose your date. Book directly with us and you pay no booking fees.

Can I cancel free of charge?

Yes, you can cancel free of charge up to 14 days before arrival.

Can I use the wellness area?

The wellness area on the Top Floor is not included in the package. You can add it at reception during your stay. Your Deluxe room has its own infrared sauna.

Can I park at the hotel?

Yes, parking at the hotel is free.
