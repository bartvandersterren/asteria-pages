Golf in De Maasduinen

From Hotel Asteria it is a 23-minute drive to Golfbaan Landgoed Bleijenbeek in Afferden, 17.4 kilometres away. The course opened in 2005 and was designed by golf course architect Gerard Jol. The estate lies in the heart of De Maasduinen National Park and is an official starting point for the park.

There are three courses: the 18-hole Championship Course, a 9-hole par 3-4 course and a pitch and putt course. There is also a driving range, practice greens and practice bunkers. Parking is free.

The courses on bleijenbeek.nl (opens in a new tab)

Avenues, water and slopes

The Championship Course measures 6,370 metres from the whites, par 73. You play past old tree-lined avenues, a stream and water features, and over the changes in elevation on the estate. Hole 5 is a 358-metre par 4 with stroke index 1: the hardest hole on the course.

On the Championship Course you need a golf proficiency certificate. For the par 3-4 course, par 29, course permission is enough, and the pitch and putt course is for recreational play. Allow about four and a half hours for 18 holes as a two-ball and five hours as a three- or four-ball.

Castle, museum and heath

Next to the course lie the castle ruins of Bleijenbeek, which you can see from the estate. Since 2016 the pavilion by the course has housed the Dutch Golf Museum (Nederlands Golfmuseum). Admission is free with a green fee. The museum starts with Pieter van Afferden, who in 1545 described a ball game played to a hole in a small Latin schoolbook.

If you fancy a walk after your round, you can head straight from the estate into the national park, with heath, woodland and river dunes. Our page on De Maasduinen National Park (in Dutch) tells you more. Rather play a second round on another course? Golfclub Geijsteren is a 21-minute drive away.

Tip from reception

Allow an extra half hour for the Dutch Golf Museum in the pavilion: admission is free with your green fee. And after your round, take a short walk to the castle ruins next to the course, especially in the low light of late afternoon.

Good to know

  • Three courses

    Championship Course: 18 holes, par 73, 6,370 metres from the whites. Plus a par 3-4 course (par 29) and a pitch and putt course.

  • Green fee

    18 holes €65 on weekdays and €73 at weekends, 9 holes €43 or €47. Rates April to November 2026, including the driving range.

  • Certificate required

    On the Championship Course you are welcome with a golf proficiency certificate. For the par 3-4 course, course permission is enough.

  • Practice and museum

    Driving range with mats for left- and right-handed players, practice greens and bunkers. The Dutch Golf Museum is in the pavilion.

How to make a day of it

Breakfast at the hotel, eighteen holes in De Maasduinen, then the sauna and a table in the restaurant.

  1. The breakfast buffet is served from 7 am to 10 am on weekdays and from 8 am to 10.30 am at weekends. Then it is a 23-minute drive to Afferden.

  2. Book your tee time in advance; with the golf package, reception arranges it for you. Allow about four and a half hours for 18 holes. Paviljoen Bleijenbeek, with its large terrace overlooking the estate, is the place for lunch or a drink.

  3. In 23 minutes you are back in Venray, and the sauna gives your legs a rest. The wellness area is open from 4 pm to 10 pm Monday to Thursday and from 12 noon to 10 pm Friday to Sunday. Tuesday is swimwear day.

  4. End the day at a table in the hotel restaurant, where you can have dinner until 9.30 pm. With the golf package, the three-course dinner is included.

Frequently asked questions

Any questions? Call reception on +31 478 511 466

How far is Golfbaan Landgoed Bleijenbeek from Hotel Asteria?

It is 17.4 kilometres to the pavilion at Bleijenbeek 14, about 23 minutes by car. By bike it is 14.5 kilometres, around 55 minutes. You can hire bikes and e-bikes at our reception.

What is included in the golf package?

A night in a Royale room with breakfast buffet, a green fee for 18 holes at Golfbaan Landgoed Bleijenbeek or Golfclub Geijsteren and a three-course dinner in the hotel restaurant. Use the button by the package to choose your date and see the price. After booking, phone reception on +31 478 511 466 to let them know which course you would like.

Do I need a golf proficiency certificate?

For the Championship Course, yes. For the par 3-4 course, course permission is enough, and the pitch and putt course is for recreational play.

How much is a green fee without the package?

From April to November 2026, 18 holes on the Championship Course cost €65 on weekdays and €73 at weekends. Nine holes cost €43 or €47. Every green fee also gives you access to the driving range and the golf museum. For the winter months, ask the course for the rate on +31 485 530084.

How do I book a tee time?

With the golf package, you phone our reception after booking with your choice of course (+31 478 511 466) and they reserve it for you. Without the package, book online via Teecontrol or call the course on +31 485 530084.

Can I also play at Geijsteren?

Yes. Golfclub Geijsteren is 12 minutes from the hotel and a 21-minute drive from Bleijenbeek. The golf package includes a green fee for one of the two courses; a round on the other course you book yourself, directly with that course.
