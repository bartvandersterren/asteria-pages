Golf on the estate

From Hotel Asteria it is a 12-minute drive to Golfclub Geijsteren, 10.6 kilometres away. The club lies on Landgoed Geijsteren, a country estate where golf has been played since the 1970s. The course was designed by the architects Donald Steel and Frank Pennink.

It started with nine holes. Four more were added in 1983 and another five water holes in 1985, and a year later the clubhouse was built. Geijsteren is a members' club, but visitors are welcome seven days a week.

The course on golfclubgeijsteren.nl (opens in a new tab)

First woods, then water

The first thirteen holes run through the middle of the woods. The last five are water holes, the part laid out in 1985. From the white tees the course measures 6,090 metres, par 72: four par 3s, four par 5s and par 4s for the rest. In 1988 it hosted the first national Matchplay Championship for professionals in the Netherlands.

On the course you wear golf shoes with spikes or soft spikes and a shirt or blouse with a collar. Jeans are fine as long as they are not ripped. Dogs are not allowed on the course, but are welcome on the terrace on a lead.

The estate and the stream

The club lies on Landgoed Geijsteren, with woodland, meadows and the Oostrumse Beek stream. The club keeps track of the wildlife on the course, from birds and butterflies to bats, and holds a GEO certificate for sustainable course management. After your round you can sit on the terrace of restaurant OffCourse, looking out over a few of the holes. Non-golfers are welcome there too. Fancy a different course the next day? Golfbaan Landgoed Bleijenbeek is a 21-minute drive away.

Tip from reception

At weekends, visitors are only welcome on the course from 11 am. If you like to play early, plan your round for a weekday morning. Or book a twilight round, from 1 pm on winter greens and 3 pm on summer greens. That way you are back in good time for dinner in the restaurant.

Good to know

  • Woods and water

    18 holes, par 72, 6,090 metres from the whites. Thirteen holes through the woods, then five water holes.

  • Green fee

    18 holes €75, on winter greens €60. Nine holes €45 or €35, twilight €60 or €50. 2026 rates.

  • Visitors welcome

    Seven days a week, from 11 am at weekends. You need at least a golf proficiency certificate (Dutch GVB), handicap 54.

  • Practice

    A 300-metre driving range, a nine-hole putting green, a practice triangle and a pitch and putt course.

How to make a day of it

Breakfast at the hotel, eighteen holes on Landgoed Geijsteren, then the sauna and a table in the restaurant.

  1. The breakfast buffet is served from 7 am to 10 am on weekdays and from 8 am to 10.30 am at weekends. At weekends you are welcome on the course from 11 am, so there is no need to rush.

  2. Book your tee time in advance; with the golf package, reception arranges it for you. After your round you can sit on the terrace of restaurant OffCourse, looking out over a few of the holes.

  3. Twelve minutes later you are back in Venray, and the sauna gives your legs a rest. The wellness area is open from 4 pm to 10 pm Monday to Thursday and from 12 noon to 10 pm Friday to Sunday. Tuesday is swimwear day.

  4. End the day at a table in the hotel restaurant, where you can have dinner until 9.30 pm. With the golf package, the three-course dinner is included.

Frequently asked questions

Any questions? Call reception on +31 478 511 466

How far is Golfclub Geijsteren from Hotel Asteria?

It is 10.6 kilometres to the clubhouse on Het Spekt, about 12 minutes by car. By bike it is 7.5 kilometres, around 30 minutes. You can hire bikes and e-bikes at our reception.

What is included in the golf package?

A night in a Royale room with breakfast buffet, a green fee for 18 holes at Golfclub Geijsteren or Golfbaan Landgoed Bleijenbeek and a three-course dinner in the hotel restaurant. Use the button by the package to choose your date and see the price. After booking, phone reception on +31 478 511 466 to let them know which course you would like.

Do I need a handicap?

Yes. As a visitor you need at least a golf proficiency certificate, handicap 54.

How much is a green fee without the package?

In 2026 you pay €75 for 18 holes, or €60 when winter greens are in play. Nine holes cost €45 or €35, a twilight round €60 or €50. A buggy costs €35 per round.

How do I book a tee time?

With the golf package, you phone our reception after booking with your choice of course (+31 478 511 466) and they reserve it for you. Without the package, book through the form on the club's website or call +31 478 532592.

Can I also play at Bleijenbeek?

Yes. Golfbaan Landgoed Bleijenbeek in Afferden is 23 minutes from the hotel and a 21-minute drive from Geijsteren. The golf package includes a green fee for one of the two courses; a round on the other course you book yourself, directly with that course.
